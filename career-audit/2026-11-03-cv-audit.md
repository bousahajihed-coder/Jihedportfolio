## 2. Critical problems

### C1. The "6+ years leading international campaigns" claim doesn't survive a date check. [Fact]

| Period | Role |
|---|---|
| Sep 2024 → | Creative Producer, AUTO1 (≈ 2 years) |
| Apr 2024 → | EP, INLEGACY (parallel) |
| Nov 2023 – Mar 2024 | Freelance EP, GMP (5 months) |
| Jan 2023 – Jan 2024 | Video Producer / Director, TBD Media (1 year) |
| Mar 2021 – Jan 2022 | Freelance camera operator |
| 2019–2020 | 2-month series, internship |
| 2019–2023 | BA in Cinematography (studying full-time) |

**What the dates show:**
- Producing roles start in **January 2023**. That is about 3 years of producing.
- About 2 years at the €500k level, at AUTO1.
- About 7 years in production overall, if you count the BA and early work.

Every MD and EP does this arithmetic.

**Why it matters [Assessment]:** "6+ years leading international campaigns" reads as inflated. Once one claim looks inflated, the reader discounts the €500k and the EP titles too.

**Fix:** say exactly what is true and strong:
> "Producing since 2023, in production since 2019; currently running campaigns with budgets up to €500k+."

That is impressive for 29. Inflated, it becomes a liability.

### C2. Your three Executive Producer entries contain no evidence. [Fact]

- **INLEGACY:** "Provide executive oversight for commercial and branded content productions …"
- **GMP:** "Delivered freelance executive producing for commercial and branded video projects, managing budgets, scheduling, and vendor coordination …"

**What's missing:** no client, no project, no budget figure, no number of productions, no shoot locations.

**Why it matters:** these are the entries that justify the EP title. Empty, they make the title look borrowed.

An EP at 26–27 is believable at a boutique studio, but only if the reader can see what you executive-produced.

### C3. No credits section. [Fact]

The CV has no list of named productions (brand / agency / role / year). This is the same gap as the website.

For freelance producers, the credits list is the CV; the experience section is supporting detail.

### C4. Your CV, website and brief pitch three different levels. [Fact]

| Source | How you are positioned |
|---|---|
| **CV** | "Creative Producer and Executive Producer", skill: "Executive Producing" |
| **Website** | "Creative Producer"; also offers **Assistant Producer** and **Production Coordinator** |
| **Website** | Never mentions AUTO1, INLEGACY, GMP or TBD Media |
| **Your brief to me** | Mentions GMP and AUTO1, not INLEGACY |

**Why it matters:** an MD who opens both your CV and your website sees an EP on one and a coordinator on the other. That inconsistency costs more than either level on its own.

## 3. Weaknesses

- **W1. The AUTO1 bullets are the right content but generic.** [Fact] "Lead end-to-end creative production …" and "Manage commercial production budgets of up to €500K+ …" are good claims.
  - **[Need]** Which brands or campaigns?
  - **[Need]** How many productions per year?
  - **[Need]** Do you hire external production companies? If yes, which?
  - That last point matters most: hiring and managing production companies is exactly what makes you credible to them.
- **W2. The summary is a keyword block.** [Fact] "Manages … provides hands-on command of the full production process." It is written in the third person and stacks every keyword. Experienced producers write three plain sentences.
- **W3. "Executive Producing" is listed as a skill.** [Fact] It's a title, not a skill. Replace it with what an EP does: bidding, budget sign-off, vendor and talent contracts, client lead.
- **W4. Old entries dilute you.** [Fact]
  - Content creator for a band (2017).
  - Camera intern in Tunis (2015).
  - A 2-month series (2019–20).
  - These take a quarter of the page. Compress them into one "Earlier" line.
  - The camera background stays as a *story* ("came up through the camera department"), not as a list of internships.
- **W5. No phone number, no website link in the header.** [Fact]
- **W6. Italian is inconsistent.** [Fact] The CV says "Elementary"; the website says "Working knowledge". Use one level everywhere. "Elementary" is the safer one.
- **W7. "Freelance / Parallel Role" at INLEGACY.** [Assessment]
  - **Good:** it proves you can freelance alongside AUTO1.
  - **Risky:** it raises "how available is he really?"
  - Have a one-line answer ready, for example "available for [X] days/month and full bookings from [date]", and put your availability on the website.
