## 6. Market positioning

### Producer vs Senior Producer vs Executive Producer, in practice

- **Producer (production company, freelance).** Joins when a job is awarded, or at bid stage. Builds or works to the bid, crews up, runs pre-production, shoot and post within the fixed price, and is the day-to-day contact for the agency producer. **This is the core freelance booking.**
- **Senior Producer.** Same job at higher budgets, more complexity (multi-country, celebrity talent, big VFX) and more autonomy. Trusted to bid and to manage the agency relationship alone.
- **Executive Producer (production company).** Wins work. Manages the director roster, advises agencies on director fit, signs off bids, and oversees several producers. Rarely booked freelance by established production companies, because it is a relationship and sales role tied to the company's roster.
- **"Freelance EP"** exists mainly in three places:
  - agencies and in-house brand teams, overseeing outsourced production;
  - content studios;
  - smaller production companies without a permanent EP.

### What keeps a freelancer on a production company's call list

1. They brought a job in on budget, and the cost report closed cleanly.
2. The agency producer asked for them again.
3. They are reachable and give a straight yes or no on dates.
4. They have their own trusted crew and vendors.
5. They make the EP's life easier, not noisier.

All of this is shown through **named credits and referrals**, not adjectives.

### Your most realistic, commercially attractive position [Assessment]

> **Producer — commercials & branded content, Berlin.**
> **Specialisms:** multi-market and multilingual productions (DE/EN/FR/AR); automotive.

**Why:**
- It is credible at 29 with your path.
- It matches the bookings that actually exist: freelance producers for production companies, agencies and brand teams.
- Your languages plus multi-market localisation are a genuine niche. International service work in Germany, MENA-market versions of European campaigns and French-speaking markets are all areas where most Berlin producers can't compete.

**Market for "Executive Producer":** agencies, brands and content studios that need someone to run outsourced production. Mention it on LinkedIn and in targeted outreach to those clients. Don't lead with it on the homepage.
