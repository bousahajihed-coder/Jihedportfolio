## WHAT I AM SEEING
1. **Advertised freelance producer roles in Berlin are rare.** Almost every Berlin producer ad found today was a staff role. Freelance work in commercials here moves through networks such as Crew United and direct contact, which is why the outreach list matters as much as the job list.
2. **"Creative Producer" plus localization is the most common freelance-friendly title.** Mozilla, Wolt, SCRL and Media.Monks all advertise contract or freelance producers whose job is multi-market adaptation and localization, a niche where your languages help.
3. **AI in production is now a stated requirement.** Wolt ("AI-enabled growth engine"), SCRL (AI creative tools) and Markenfilm ("interest in AI") all mention it. A line about your AI-assisted workflow, if you have one, would help applications.
4. **Remote Europe contracts are coming from tech brands, not production companies.** Mozilla, Wolt and SCRL all offer contracts you could do from Berlin, while production companies still want people on-site.
